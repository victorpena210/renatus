The method, in plain language
Know what the result can—and can’t—tell you.
What this looks at
Annualize separate external costs, then optionally value your team’s time. Identify responsibilities that may not be covered by your arrangement.
What to do with the result
Compare proposals by actual scope: updates, recovery, form testing, response times, edits, ownership, and fees.
Limits and assumptions
Unknown charges are never zero. The tool does not directly compare a maintenance-only arrangement with Renatus’s build subscription or declare a provider overpriced.
Checklist scoring: Yes = 2, Partly = 1, No = 0. Unknown answers are excluded. The percentage uses only known answers, with equal weight per question. At least half the checklist must be known before an overall score is shown. Always read the evidence coverage beside the score.
Method reviewed 2026-09-29. This preliminary tool does not replace an individually scoped technical, security, financial, or professional assessment.
