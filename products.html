Turn employee schedules into shuttle plans.

RoutePulse is being developed for organizations that operate employee transportation. It uses workforce schedules to help predict pickup demand, organize time windows, plan driver coverage, and tell riders whether shuttle service is currently running.

  • Workforce schedule import
  • Pickup-window and demand planning
  • Driver scheduling and assignments
  • Employee-facing shuttle service status
  • Optional GPS tracking planned
