About Renatus

Hi, I’m Victor Peña. I build technology around real problems.

Renatus is a San Antonio technology studio that I own and operate. I build custom websites, business software, portals, dashboards, workflow systems, and subscription-based digital products.

Web design is one part of Renatus, but it is not the limit of the company. You can bring me an operational problem, a process that depends too heavily on spreadsheets and manual work, or an idea for a new digital product. I will help define it, design it, build it, and turn it into technology people can actually use.

I am also developing Renatus subscription products. RoutePulse is designed around employee transportation operations, workforce schedules, pickup demand, and driver coverage. SuiteFlow is being developed as a branded tenant portal for property owners, managers, and the people they serve.

Before starting Renatus, I helped maintain WordPress and Joomla websites at an advertising agency. Today, clients work directly with me—from understanding the problem and planning the solution through development, launch, support, and continued improvement.
