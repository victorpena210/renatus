Software Product • In Development

RoutePulse

RoutePulse is employee shuttle planning software designed around workforce schedules. It is being built to help transportation teams predict pickup demand before employees clock out, organize service windows, plan driver coverage, and keep riders informed.

  • Workforce schedule import and pickup-window planning
  • Low, medium, and high demand forecasting
  • Driver scheduling and assignments
  • Employee-facing “Is the shuttle running?” status
  • Optional GPS tracking and live arrival visibility

RoutePulse is being developed as a Renatus product for theme parks, attractions, campuses, resorts, warehouses, and other shift-based workplaces that operate employee transportation.
