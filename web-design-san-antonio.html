San Antonio Web Design FAQs

Straight answers to common questions from small business owners.

How much does a small business website cost?

Renatus's website subscription is $299 per month with an initial 12-month commitment, for a total initial cost of $3,588. It includes a custom 1–5 page website, managed hosting, security, reasonable content edits, and email support. After the initial term, service continues month-to-month. Larger custom projects start at $3,500.

What is included with my website?

Every site is custom-built for your business, designed for mobile devices, and organized around clear customer actions. Subscription plans also include hosting, security, updates, and support.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Renatus can replace an outdated, slow, or confusing website with a cleaner site that better explains your business and makes it easier for visitors to contact you.

Is SEO included?

SEO fundamentals are included: descriptive titles and metadata, semantic headings, mobile-friendly layouts, clean code, and indexing files. Rankings also depend on competition, useful content, reviews, links, and your Google Business Profile.

Do you only work with San Antonio businesses?

No. Renatus is based in San Antonio and enjoys working directly with local owners, but remote website projects are also welcome.

What happens if I cancel my website subscription?

After the initial 12-month commitment is completed, the subscription continues month-to-month. If you end service before the initial term is complete, the remaining balance of the initial commitment is due.

Can I take my website somewhere else?

Yes. Once the initial subscription commitment has been paid in full, you may request the final static website files and move the website to another hosting provider. Migration or configuration work with a replacement provider may be quoted separately.

How do I get started?

Send a short description of your business, current website, and goals. Victor will review it and follow up within one business day to discuss the right next step.
