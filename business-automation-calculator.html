The method, in plain language
Know what the result can—and can’t—tell you.
What this looks at
Choose one task and enter its frequency, duration, and labor cost. Model a percentage reduction and the costs required to achieve it.
What to do with the result
Compare a simpler process, an existing feature, an integration, and custom software. Decide whether saved time can actually be redeployed before calling it cash savings.
Limits and assumptions
Annual hours = minutes × weekly occurrences × working weeks ÷ 60. Modeled labor value = annual hours × hourly cost × reduction. Costs are subtracted separately; no revenue uplift is assumed.
Method reviewed 2026-09-29. This preliminary tool does not replace an individually scoped technical, security, financial, or professional assessment.
