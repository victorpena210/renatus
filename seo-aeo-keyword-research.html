Three Parts of a Better Search Strategy

Keyword research identifies the opportunity. SEO and AEO help turn that opportunity into useful, understandable website content.

Keyword Research

Find the words, services, problems, locations, and questions potential customers use when they search. The goal is to understand intent—not to place the same phrase everywhere.

Search Engine Optimization

Give every important page a clear topic, descriptive title, logical headings, helpful copy, internal links, and a technically sound foundation that search engines can crawl and index.

Answer Engine Optimization

Organize information into direct explanations, useful questions, and specific answers. This makes the content easier for people, search engines, and AI-powered tools to understand and summarize.

A Practical Four-Step Process

The work begins with your customers and ends with a website that communicates your services more clearly.

1. Learn the Business

Review your services, ideal customers, service area, competitors, current website, and the actions you want visitors to take.

2. Research Searches

Find relevant keywords, specific questions, local searches, and longer phrases that reveal what potential customers need.

3. Plan or Improve Pages

Decide which pages are needed, assign topics to them, and improve their headings, copy, metadata, links, and calls to action.

4. Publish and Refine

Launch the improvements, verify that pages can be discovered, and use future search data to guide additional content updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about search visibility and what the work can realistically accomplish.

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO helps search engines discover, understand, and rank website pages. AEO focuses on making information clear and direct so search engines and AI-powered tools can identify useful answers to specific questions.

What does keyword research include?

It examines the services, problems, locations, and questions potential customers search for. Those findings can become service pages, location pages, FAQs, and future content topics.

Can SEO guarantee a number-one Google ranking?

No ethical provider can guarantee a particular position. Renatus improves structure, relevance, technical foundations, and visibility without promising a specific ranking.

How quickly will SEO work?

Search visibility usually develops over time. The pace depends on the website, competition, location, search demand, content, and other factors outside any one provider’s control.
