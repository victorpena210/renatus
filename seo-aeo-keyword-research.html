Keyword research identifies the opportunity. SEO and AEO help turn that opportunity into useful, understandable website content.

Find the words, services, problems, locations, and questions potential customers use when they search. The goal is to understand intent—not to place the same phrase everywhere.

Search Engine Optimization

Give every important page a clear topic, descriptive title, logical headings, helpful copy, internal links, and a technically sound foundation that search engines can crawl and index.

Answer Engine Optimization

Organize information into direct explanations, useful questions, and specific answers. This makes the content easier for people, search engines, and AI-powered tools to understand and summarize.

How I Approach Search Visibility

A Practical SEO & AEO Process

I use a research-first process built around the same fundamentals that drive effective SEO: choosing worthwhile searches, matching intent, creating useful pages, strengthening authority, keeping the site technically healthy, and improving it with real performance data.

1. Understand the Business

I start with your services, customers, service area, competitors, goals, and the actions you want visitors to take. Search work only matters when it supports the business behind the website.

2. Research Keywords & Search Intent

I look for searches with real demand and business value, then evaluate traffic potential, search intent, competing results, and realistic ranking difficulty before deciding what to target.

3. Build or Improve the Right Pages

I map useful topics to specific pages and improve titles, headings, copy, internal links, calls to action, and content depth so each page satisfies what the searcher is actually trying to do.

4. Add the AEO Layer

Important questions are answered clearly and directly. I organize content with descriptive headings, concise explanations, FAQs, structured data, and specific business details that are easier for search engines and AI-powered tools to understand and cite.

5. Strengthen Technical SEO & Authority

I review crawlability, indexing, canonical URLs, sitemaps, mobile usability, structured data, and site architecture, while also identifying relevant opportunities for legitimate links, mentions, citations, and other signals of real-world authority.

6. Measure, Learn & Refine

After publishing, I use Search Console, analytics, rankings, and referral data to see what people are finding, which pages are earning visibility, and where the next improvement should happen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about search visibility and what the work can realistically accomplish.

What is the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO helps search engines discover, understand, and rank website pages. AEO focuses on making information clear and direct so search engines and AI-powered tools can identify useful answers to specific questions.

What does keyword research include?

It examines the services, problems, locations, and questions potential customers search for. Those findings can become service pages, location pages, FAQs, and future content topics.

Can SEO guarantee a number-one Google ranking?

No ethical provider can guarantee a particular position. Renatus improves structure, relevance, technical foundations, and visibility without promising a specific ranking.

How quickly will SEO work?

Search visibility usually develops over time. The pace depends on the website, competition, location, search demand, content, and other factors outside any one provider’s control.
