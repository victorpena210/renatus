A few helpful answers

Before you
come aboard.

Does it assign driving shifts?

No. The current product records actual driving and service status. Earlier planning tools are retired, and existing history is preserved.

Do riders need an account or app?

No. Riders open the public link or scan the QR code shared by their organization. The page shows status and the active driver’s first name; there is no ride-confirmation step.

Does it predict arrival times?

No. Status reflects recorded driver actions and manager overrides. It does not provide public GPS tracking or arrival predictions. A forgotten run stays active until a driver or manager ends it.

Is the product available now?

Is the Shuttle Running is in demo phase at istheshuttlerunning.com. Demo and pilot access are reviewed for each organization. A request does not create an account.
