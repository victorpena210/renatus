A few helpful answers

Before you
come aboard.

Where is the product being piloted?

Is the Shuttle Running? is being piloted at Six Flags Fiesta Texas in San Antonio, Texas.

How does UKG schedule syncing help drivers?

Drivers connect their UKG access so their work schedules sync automatically and can be used for shuttle driver assignments.

How does the app help plan pickups for employees with mobility impairments?

The team can view scheduled clock-out times and use them to plan pickup assistance around employees’ shift endings.

What can managers review?

Managers can filter driving history by date and driver, review start and end times, and see actual driving-time totals. Paused time is excluded.

Do riders need an account or app?

No. Riders open the public link or scan the QR code shared by their organization. The page shows whether service is running and the active driver’s first name.

How is shuttle status updated?

Driver starts, pauses, handoffs, and endings update recorded service. Managers can also control service status. Riders see running or not running and the active driver’s first name.

How can my organization get started?

Contact Renatus for a product walkthrough and organization setup. Driver and manager access is configured for your team.
