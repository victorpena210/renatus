What Does Website Maintenance Cover?

A website needs attention when your hours, services, staff, photos, or customer contact details change. The right support also depends on the technology behind the site and the tools it connects to.

Content Updates

Website Maintenance Questions

Is web hosting the same as website maintenance?

No. Hosting delivers your website to visitors. Maintenance covers keeping the site current and addressing issues within the agreed scope. The Renatus website subscription combines hosting, routine maintenance, and up to 30 minutes of content edits per month.

Can you review a site built by someone else?

Yes. Start with a website review. The platform, code, account access, integrations, and requested changes determine whether a cleanup project or ongoing support is appropriate.

Does a static website still need maintenance?

Yes. Its content, forms, links, domain, hosting, and connected services still need attention. A static site usually has fewer CMS and plugin updates. Compare static websites and WordPress.

What falls outside included content edits?

New pages, major redesigns, new forms, booking systems, e-commerce, portals, integrations, extensive copywriting, and ongoing SEO campaigns are quoted separately. Read the complete inclusions and exclusions.

Can I move my subscription website later?

After the initial commitment is paid in full, you may request the final static website files and move to another host. Ending service during the first 12 months requires paying the remaining initial commitment. Migration work may be quoted separately.
