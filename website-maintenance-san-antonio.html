What care includes—and what needs a quote.

Care is optional and month-to-month; cancel before the next renewal. The 30-minute allowance covers updates to existing text, photos, hours, prices, and contact details. Unused editing time does not roll over. New pages, features, redesigns, and major repairs are quoted separately before work starts.

Routine checks are separate from the editing allowance. Support is available Monday–Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays, in Central Time. The two-business-day target is for an initial reply, not a guaranteed resolution time or 24/7 coverage. Existing sites are reviewed for compatibility before care begins.

Care covers the agreed website. Third-party subscriptions, domain registration, software licenses, and processing fees are separate. Maintenance for custom applications with accounts and databases is not included.

These prices apply to new proposals. Existing clients keep their agreed pricing, scope, and payment terms unless a change is agreed in writing. The previous website subscription is paused for new customers.

Need a fix before ongoing care?

Focused website improvements are $350–$1,000 per scoped project. Send the website address and describe the problem; we review it and quote before making changes. A new small-business website is $3,500.
