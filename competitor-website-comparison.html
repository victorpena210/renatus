The method, in plain language
Know what the result can—and can’t—tell you.
What this looks at
Each homepage is requested separately and assessed with the same public HTML checks. Observations appear side by side, without an overall business winner.
What to do with the result
Use differences as questions for a human review. A metadata tag is not proof of better leads, rankings, design, or service quality.
Limits and assumptions
This does not measure traffic, conversions, reviews, vulnerabilities, rankings, indexed pages, or revenue. Failed retrievals remain unavailable, never a zero score.
