The Problem

Shuttle demand changes with the workforce.

When employees start and end shifts at different times and locations, transportation teams can end up reacting to demand after it has already formed. RoutePulse is designed to use the employee schedule as the starting point so teams can see pressure before the rush begins.

01

Know when demand is coming

Use shift start and end times to estimate when groups of employees will need transportation.

02

Plan driver coverage earlier

Organize demand into pickup windows and identify periods that may need additional coverage.

03

Give riders a clear answer

Let employees quickly see whether shuttle service is running, delayed, paused, or out of service.

RoutePulse FAQ

Questions about the product.

What is RoutePulse?

RoutePulse is employee shuttle planning software in development by Renatus. It is being built to turn workforce schedules into demand forecasts, pickup windows, driver plans, and rider-facing service information.

Does RoutePulse include GPS tracking?

GPS tracking is planned as an optional feature. The core product is designed around schedule-driven planning and service status, while a live tier can add vehicle location and arrival visibility.

Will employees need an app?

The planned rider experience is web-based and mobile-friendly so organizations can share a simple link or QR code. A dedicated mobile app is not required for the core concept.

Can RoutePulse connect to workforce scheduling systems?

Workforce-system integrations are part of the longer-term product direction. Early versions can use imported schedule data while integrations are developed for larger deployments.

Is RoutePulse available now?

RoutePulse is currently in development. Organizations interested in testing the product can request early access below.
