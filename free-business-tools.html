What happens after a check?

You can use the recommendations yourself, save a text report, or print the result. Contact details are optional. When you submit a review request, Renatus receives the answers and result you choose to share so the conversation can start with context.

These tools do not connect to your Google accounts, monitor search rankings, certify security or accessibility, or promise financial outcomes. Automated homepage checks use public HTML and may be unavailable when a site blocks retrieval. Read each tool’s method before acting on its result.
