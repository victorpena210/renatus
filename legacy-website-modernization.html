Legacy websites & migration

Modernize what needs to change. Preserve what works.

Review support, access, content, URLs, and integrations before replacing an older website.

Check your modernization prioritiesDiscuss your project

A website’s age alone does not establish that it needs replacement. Renatus reviews the problems you can demonstrate: unsupported software, unreliable forms, difficult editing, inaccurate content, or a fragile integration. The right answer may be a repair, a staged migration, or a rebuild—but that decision should follow the evidence.

01 / The workflow

Inventory before changing infrastructure

Identify the domain, hosting, source or export rights, important URLs, content, forms, analytics, and integrations. Establish who controls each account and which provider responsibilities remain active. Take an appropriate backup and define what a successful restoration would look like before changing the live environment.

02 / The workflow

Preserve useful content and journeys

Record important landing pages and the actions customers currently complete. Map old URLs to relevant replacements and keep service information accurate. Replacing every page with a new homepage is not a migration plan. Search performance, form behavior, and analytics continuity need explicit acceptance checks.

03 / The workflow

Launch with a rollback path

Use a review environment to test navigation, forms, redirects, and critical handoffs. Agree on the cutover steps, the person who can reverse them, and the observations that would trigger a rollback. After launch, review real requests and errors rather than treating a successful deployment message as the end of the project.

A sensible first scope

The initial engagement can be an inventory and migration plan. Implementation scope follows the confirmed systems, content, and risks rather than a fixed assumption that every older site requires a full rebuild.

Every proposal should identify deliverables, exclusions, third-party costs, acceptance tests, and ongoing responsibilities. Review Renatus pricing and engagement options.
