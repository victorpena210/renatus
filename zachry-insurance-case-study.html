The Challenge

Make a complex service feel clear and trustworthy.

Zachry Insurance needed more than a polished homepage. Life insurance can feel complicated and overly sales-driven, so the website had to build trust, explain coverage in approachable language, and help people take the next step without pressure.

The business also needed to serve two different audiences. Prospective clients needed educational tools and clear ways to request guidance, while prospective agents needed a separate path to understand the opportunity and submit their information.

The goal was one digital foundation for brand trust, customer education, structured lead capture, agent recruiting, and measurable growth.
  • Establish a distinctive new insurance brand with a credible, consistent visual system.
  • Explain protection needs for new parents, families, and people planning for final expenses.
  • Capture policy-review and contact requests in a structured format.
  • Give prospective agents a clear recruiting and application path.
  • Include search, accessibility, privacy, and analytics foundations from the beginning.

The Solution

A custom website built for education and action.

Renatus combined the public-facing brand with interactive guidance, structured forms, recruiting, and measurement behind the scenes.

01

Brand & Custom Website

Created a distinctive navy-and-gold identity centered on the resting dalmatian, then carried that visual system into a responsive custom website designed to feel established, calm, and approachable.

02

Structured Lead Generation

Built clear conversion paths for policy reviews, general inquiries, and other customer needs, with custom forms that organize submissions for follow-up instead of relying on unstructured email messages.

03

Interactive Coverage Tools

Developed guided experiences including a Policy Decoder, Parent Coverage Wizard, AI Coverage Interview, and Final Expense Planner to help visitors better understand their needs before speaking with an agent.

04

Recruiting & Measurement

Added an agent-recruiting path, structured application forms, search optimization, analytics events, and first-touch attribution so the business can evaluate how prospects find and use the website.

The Client Journey

One clear path from uncertainty to guidance.

The experience helps visitors learn at their own pace and choose the next step that fits their situation.

Step 01

Discover the Brand

Visitors arrive at a focused, professional website with a consistent identity and a clear explanation of how Zachry Insurance can help.

Step 02

Explore Their Needs

Content and planning paths address real situations, including family protection, coverage questions, and final-expense planning.

Step 03

Use a Guided Tool

Interactive tools turn broad insurance questions into a more useful, personalized starting point for a conversation with an agent.

Step 04

Request Guidance

The visitor can submit an organized request for help, while agent candidates can follow a separate recruiting and application journey.

The Outcome

A credible digital foundation for clients and agency growth.

Zachry Insurance now has a live, mobile-friendly website that brings branding, education, lead capture, recruiting, and measurement into one system. Visitors can understand the business, explore coverage needs, use guided tools, and request help without navigating a disconnected collection of pages and forms.
