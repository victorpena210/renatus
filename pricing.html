How monthly support works

Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays, in Central Time. Response targets mean an initial reply and triage, not a guaranteed resolution time or 24/7 coverage.

Monthly edit and work allowances do not roll over. Requests are handled one at a time. Work beyond an allowance, new pages, redesigns and new functionality are quoted for approval before work begins. Website check results include recommendations; larger repairs are separately scoped.

Website care includes the agreed site's hosting and SSL. Software hosting, databases, monitoring services, third-party subscriptions and usage charges are separate unless listed in the proposal. Eligibility, supported components, access and recovery responsibilities are confirmed before support starts; initial repairs and migrations are separate.

Care plans renew month-to-month and can be canceled before the next renewal. New website subscriptions have the separate 12-month initial commitment below. These published plans apply to new proposals; existing clients keep their agreed terms unless a change is agreed in writing.

QUESTIONS / BEFORE WE START

Know what you’re buying.

What does custom software cost at Renatus?

Custom software projects typically begin at $20,000. A narrowly scoped internal tool can start at $12,500; business operations platforms start at $35,000. More complex multi-user or multi-tenant platforms are generally planned at $45,000–$75,000+. A written proposal establishes the actual price and deliverables.

What changes the final project price?

The number of workflows, user roles, business rules, integrations, reporting needs and data migrations affects the work. We also define testing, deployment, documentation and handover. New requirements receive an agreed scope and price before implementation.

Is discovery included in the build price?

Software discovery and architecture is a separate $2,500–$5,000 engagement when needed. Its fee, deliverables and any credit toward implementation are agreed in writing before work begins. An initial project conversation is free.

Why choose a one-time website project?

A one-time project offers a tailored page structure, content organization and two design revision rounds, with final files after payment in full and optional ongoing care. The subscription is limited to 1–5 informational pages using approved content, one inquiry form and one revision round, with a 12-month commitment. Choose based on the scope and payment arrangement you need.

How are payments and ownership handled?

The proposal sets milestones, acceptance criteria, payment dates, source-code ownership, third-party licenses and handover requirements. We agree on those details before development. Website subscription terms are listed below.

What is the difference between Website Care and Software Care?

Website Care Essentials is $149/month with 30 minutes of content edits; Care Plus is $299/month with 90 minutes of edits and monthly journey and tracking checks. Software Care starts at $750/month with up to 5 maintenance hours. Priority software support offers up to 10 hours for $1,500/month or 20 hours for $3,000/month, shared between maintenance and development.
