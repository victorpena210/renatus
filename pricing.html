Care is optional and month-to-month; cancel before the next renewal. The 30-minute allowance covers updates to existing text, photos, hours, prices, and contact details. Routine checks are separate from the editing allowance. Support is available Monday–Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays, in Central Time.
