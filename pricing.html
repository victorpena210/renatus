How the Subscription Works

Know exactly what you're paying for and what happens if you decide to leave.

1. Initial 12-Month Term

The website subscription is $299 per month for an initial 12-month commitment, for a total initial cost of $3,588.

2. After 12 Months

After the initial 12 months 12 months are completed, the subscription continues month-to-month at $299 per month until canceled.

3. If You Cancel Early

You may end the subscription before the initial term is complete by paying the remaining balance of the 12-month commitment.

4. If You Leave Renatus

Once the initial commitment has been paid in full, you may request the final static website files and move the website to another hosting provider.

What You Own

Your Domain

Your business should own and control its domain name. Renatus can help connect it to your website without taking ownership of the domain.

Your Content

You retain ownership of the logos, photographs, written content, trademarks, and other original materials you provide.

Your Website Files

After the initial subscription commitment has been paid in full, you may request the final static website files and host or modify them elsewhere.

Renatus Tools

Renatus retains its reusable development tools, code libraries, workflows, templates, techniques, and general-purpose components.
