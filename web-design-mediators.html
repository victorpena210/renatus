Mediation practices

A better booking journey for mediators

Connect service information, appointment selection, intake, and payment handoffs around the way your practice actually works.

Review a repetitive booking taskDiscuss your project

Booking a mediation can involve more than one participant, different session lengths, availability rules, and separate payment responsibilities. Renatus maps the complete journey before choosing which parts belong on the website and which belong in a scheduling or payment platform. The practice remains responsible for reviewing its professional policies and service terms.

01 / The workflow

Explain the appointment before the calendar

Help visitors distinguish session types, duration, pricing structure, participation requirements, and the information needed to request or schedule a session. Use practice-approved language. Make it clear whether the next action creates a confirmed booking or only submits a request for review.

02 / The workflow

Map multi-party handoffs explicitly

Document who schedules, who receives notifications, and whether each party has a separate payment obligation. Include cancellation, rescheduling, duplicate notifications, and unsuccessful payment events in the implementation plan. Automation should reduce repetitive coordination without making assumptions about a case or overriding the practice’s decisions.

03 / The workflow

Test the workflow, not just the landing page

Exercise the appointment types you actually offer, the notice period, confirmation messages, meeting links, and payment handoffs. Keep secrets on the server and define a manual recovery route when an outside provider is unavailable. A successful payment-link redirect alone is not proof that every participant’s obligation was completed.

A sensible first scope

Start with one complete appointment journey. Additional session types, specialized intake, revised payment rules, and account-based access can be planned as separate releases.

Every proposal should identify deliverables, exclusions, third-party costs, acceptance tests, and ongoing responsibilities. Review Renatus pricing and engagement options.
