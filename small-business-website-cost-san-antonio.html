What the $3,500 package includes.

  • Up to five pages: Home, About, Services, Contact, and one agreed page
  • Custom design for phones, tablets, and desktop screens
  • One inquiry form, tested from submission to delivery
  • Basic search setup, analytics, and inquiry tracking
  • Light editing and organization of your supplied text and images
  • Two consolidated design revision rounds
  • Launch, final website files after full payment, and a handoff walkthrough
  • 30 days of fixes for defects in the agreed work after launch

Additional pages, new branding, substantial copywriting, online stores, migrations, and complex integrations receive a separate written quote. Third-party subscriptions, domain registration, software licenses, and payment-processing fees are separate. Basic search setup does not include ongoing SEO campaigns or a guarantee of rankings or sales.

How payment and ownership work.

The $3,500 website package is paid in two installments: $1,750 to start and $1,750 before launch. We agree on the pages, deliverables, revisions, and responsibilities before work begins. Any additional work receives a separate price for approval first.

You keep control of your domain and business content. After full payment, you receive the final website files and can arrange your own hosting or choose optional care. Third-party components remain subject to their licenses.

These prices apply to new proposals. Existing clients keep their agreed pricing, scope, and payment terms unless a change is agreed in writing. The previous website subscription is paused for new customers.
