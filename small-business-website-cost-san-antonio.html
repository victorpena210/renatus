One-Time Option

What Is Included in a Project Starting at $3,500?

Hosting, maintenance, and ongoing content work are quoted separately instead of being included automatically.

Software subscriptions, licenses, transaction fees, hosting, and other vendor charges are separate unless the written quote specifically includes them.

What Changes the Price?

Factors That Affect Website Cost

Website pricing changes when the project requires more content, functionality, integrations, design work, or ongoing support.

Number of Pages

A focused service-business website generally costs less than a large site with many service, location, team, or educational pages.

Content and Copywriting

Extensive research, writing, editing, photography, or custom visual assets can increase the project scope.

Forms and Functionality

Advanced intake forms, calculators, customer accounts, databases, portals, or custom applications require additional development.

Scheduling and Payments

Booking systems, calendars, payment processing, invoices, subscriptions, and automated confirmations may require separate services and third-party accounts.

SEO and Content Growth

SEO foundations are included, but ongoing keyword research, new pages, content creation, and monthly optimization are separate services.

Ongoing Support

Hosting, maintenance, content edits, analytics review, integrations, and technical support affect the ongoing cost after launch.

Clear Expectations

What Is Usually Quoted Separately?

Content edits to an existing subscription website are different from building new pages, redesigning the site, or adding new functionality.

Usually Included as a Content Edit

  • Changing text on an existing page
  • Replacing an existing photograph
  • Updating business hours
  • Changing contact information
  • Updating an existing price or service
  • Updating staff information
  • Changing an existing button or link
  • Small formatting corrections

Usually Quoted Separately

  • New pages
  • Major page redesigns
  • New forms or custom functionality
  • Booking and scheduling systems
  • E-commerce
  • Customer portals or web applications
  • Third-party integrations
  • Logo or brand design
  • Extensive copywriting
  • Ongoing SEO or advertising campaigns
  • Third-party software fees
