The method, in plain language
Know what the result can—and can’t—tell you.
What this looks at
Separate one-time investment from monthly benefits and running costs. You control every dollar and time assumption.
What to do with the result
Use break-even to decide what needs validation. Compare off-the-shelf products or process changes before committing to a build.
Limits and assumptions
Net monthly benefit = hours × hourly value + other savings − running costs. ROI = (net benefit over the period − project cost) ÷ project cost. With a zero project cost, percentage ROI is undefined.
