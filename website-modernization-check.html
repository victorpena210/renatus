The method, in plain language
Know what the result can—and can’t—tell you.
What this looks at
An older site can still be effective. Focus on platform support, working journeys, accurate information, editing, ownership, and integrations.
What to do with the result
Separate cosmetic preferences from maintenance or usability problems. Consider repairing a specific weakness before replacing everything.
Limits and assumptions
The rebuild year is supplied by you; no domain-age lookup is performed. Support status is self-reported and should be checked with the actual vendor.
Checklist scoring: Yes = 2, Partly = 1, No = 0. Unknown answers are excluded. The percentage uses only known answers, with equal weight per question. At least half the checklist must be known before an overall score is shown. Always read the evidence coverage beside the score.
